    4. Interpretation and limitations

    The expected Timer 1 period is 768 6502 cycles (VIA T1 latch $02FE plus two 6522 counter cycles). Mode 1 intentionally generates no IRQ-driven R8 writes; its 64 interrupts can only be confirmed from the BASIC result record or the raw RAM samples at $6E00. Modes 2–3 should each generate 64 alternating R8 writes with a nominal 768-cycle interval.

    R8 event-to-event spacing measures the combined variability of IRQ entry, ROM dispatch, and the R8-commit point. The assembly also records entry/exit Timer 1 phase samples at $6E00/$6C00 (mode 1), $6E40/$6C40 (mode 2), and $6E80/$6C80 (mode 3). These raw bytes are not included in the Mockingboard history export; their complete interpretation requires a RAM dump. Handler interior timing excludes ROM IRQ dispatch and the RTI epilogue.

    Pass/fail uses a provisional ±12-cycle R8 spacing tolerance to reveal unexpected delays. That tolerance is a diagnostic threshold, not a verified real-hardware specification. This tool does not claim physical 6522 hardware validation.
