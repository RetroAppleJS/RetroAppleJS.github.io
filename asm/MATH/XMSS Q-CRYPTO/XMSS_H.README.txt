XMSS_HASH for Apple II+
=======================

BRUN XMSS_HASH runs the built-in address and hash primitive self-tests.

Load:   $6000
Length: $0F53

The public API and fixed buffers are documented at the top of XMSS_HASH.S.
Use XMSS_H_MSG_BEGIN / UPDATE / FINAL for messages split across multiple
chunks. XMSS_H_MSG is a one-shot 16-bit-length convenience wrapper.

Files:
  XMSS_HASH.S
  XMSS_HASH.LISTING
  XMSS_HASH.BIN
  XMSS_HASH.SYM
  XMSS_HASH.TESTS.txt
