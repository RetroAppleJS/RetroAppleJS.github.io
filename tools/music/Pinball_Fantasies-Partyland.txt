  - Control/dispatch is entirely local; no indirect calls beyond the single self-modified absolute JSR.
- Tentative/external conditions:
  - None required; no dependency on timers, CIA, or readbacks in the observed path.

AY/PAY simplifications and audible limitations
- Retain:
  - A binary “gate” state that flips every tick of PAY’s IRQ (i.e., per frame), mapping SID control toggling semantics.
- Replace/remove:
  - SID-specific waveform selection (PULSE bit) does not map meaningfully without frequency/pulse width. On AY, tone/noise enable plus amplitude are the controls; there is no concept of SID GATE with zero frequency.
  - Since no pitch is ever set, producing silence is the closest audible-faithful outcome. Any tonal output on AY would be an addition not evidenced by the SID.
- Recommended PAY-Light behavior:
  - Do not emit tone or noise; optionally toggle a muted channel’s state for bookkeeping only.
  - If a diagnostic/audible option is desired (non-faithful), make it a compile-time switch: map “on” frames to a very short, low-volume tick (e.g., pulse amplitude 1 on channel A for 1 IRQ) to emulate the clicky nature, but document this as an approximation.
