Limits

This compiler uses a compact, read-only 16-bit delta-event score and emits a finite approximation of observed AY frame states, not a proven score or full song loop. It accepts three voices / SID1 and up to 65,534 observed frames (actual capacity also depends on Apple II RAM). Compact 256-byte score pages replace per-event executable routines; original source pattern grammar and genuine musical loops are not established. Default output mutes on the first tick beyond the captured trace; repeat-observed is an explicit non-authentic audition feature. SID analog envelopes, filters and PWM are not reproduced. The diagnostic mode is not source-faithful.
