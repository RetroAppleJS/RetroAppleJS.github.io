Technical interpretation

Audio-rate envelope limitation: At a 1,021,800 Hz clock, a repeating saw reaches only about 249.5 Hz and a full triangle only about 124.7 Hz at envelope period 1. A higher requested pitch is clamped, not achieved. AY envelope shapes are 16-level stepped signals, affected by the real AY DAC curve.

Two-chip experiment: AY0 channel A is rendered on the left and AY1 channel A on the right, like the production Mockingboard; the plotted mono waveform averages those stereo outputs. Each AY has one shared envelope and one shared noise source.

Boundaries: This bench tests only the existing RetroAppleJS AYCore. It does not replace its DAC or waveform synthesis, perform a SID comparison, or measure physical hardware. The phase-1 goal is to quantify genuinely available AY timbres before designing SID-to-AY voice allocation.
