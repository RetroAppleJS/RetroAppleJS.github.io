Audacity inspection checklist

  1. Import three *_C4_probe_AYCore.wav files as separate mono tracks (File → Import → Audio).
  2. Keep them at 22,050 Hz; do not normalize, resample or apply effects before comparison.
  3. Zoom to approximately 0.140–0.155 s; each C4 cycle is about 3.8–4.0 ms.
  4. Square: two broad tone levels with filtered transitions. Saw: 16-step rising/falling envelope with a reset edge. Triangle: rising and falling envelope staircases.
  5. Inspect Analyze → Plot Spectrum using a central steady-state selection. Square/triangle tend toward odd harmonics; saw contains even and odd harmonics, but the AY DAC/filter will alter their strengths.
  6. Consult the exported JSON for each register period, actual pitch and detuning: do not infer pitch matching from the file names.

Scope: These are host-rendered AYCore reference WAVs at programmed register cycles. They do not capture Apple's 6502 bus-write latency, the browser's final AudioContext resampling, or physical AY output. The original register score is exact, while the four monophonic timbres are new offline arrangements and not E1 binaries for execution on the Apple II.
