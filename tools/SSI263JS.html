Chip parameters

Phoneme mode 3 is latched at power up. The duration control then sets DR1–DR0 for each phoneme. No oversampling.

Acoustic targets and register map

All 64 acoustic targets come from the pinned Casso source. F1–F3 are the unscaled targets; the filter register scales the tract between 0.5× and 2×. VA and FA are independent voiced and fricative levels.

This is Casso’s hardware-informed synthesis approximation. Matching its output does not establish an exact match to a physical SSI-263. The lab renders the chip output directly; it does not apply Casso’s card mixer, DC blocker, or master gain.
